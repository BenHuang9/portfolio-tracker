REAL MARKET DATA SETUP (V2.2)

1. Keep GitHub Pages hosting the app as you do now.
2. Create one free Cloudflare Worker.
3. Paste the contents of market-worker.js into the Worker and deploy it.
4. Copy the Worker URL, for example:
   https://portfolio-market-data.your-name.workers.dev
5. Open the Portfolio app -> Settings -> Market data API.
6. Paste the Worker URL -> Save & refresh.

The app will then refresh:
- stock/ETF title
- current market price
- previous close and today's move
- USD/CAD using Yahoo ticker CAD=X
- historical prices for the main chart
- dividend/distribution history available from Yahoo
- trailing annual distribution estimate

IMPORTANT
- Yahoo Finance chart access used here is unofficial and can change or be rate-limited.
- Some Canadian quotes are delayed.
- Yahoo dividend event dates are ex-dividend dates, not broker payment dates. The app therefore treats historical dividend cash as an estimate based on the shares held on each ex-date.
- The app does not connect to a brokerage. Lots remain stored locally on your device.
